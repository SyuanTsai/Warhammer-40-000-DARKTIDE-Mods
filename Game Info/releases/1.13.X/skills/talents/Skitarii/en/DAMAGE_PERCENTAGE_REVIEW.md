# Skitarii: percentage and example review

[繁體中文](../DAMAGE_PERCENTAGE_REVIEW.md) | [Player descriptions](README.md) | [Technical index](SOURCE_INDEX.md)

Distinguish one unit of Capacitance from all charges, natural regeneration rate from direct restoration, maximum Toughness from its deficit, additive layers from separate multipliers, and additional Weakspot Damage from the whole Damage amount. These are static checks against the fixed source; no in-game test was performed.

| Talent | Category | Review | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Integrated Refraction Emitter](cryptic_grenade_ability_force_field.md) | Blitz | Static check | One charge costs 75 resource with regeneration 1/s: 75 seconds to restore one, 75 × 3 = 225 seconds to restore all three without other modifiers. Their progress is shared, not three simultaneous cooldowns. Field duration is 8 seconds, with 5-metre Electrocution explosions at activation and expiry. |
| [Purgator Servo-Skull](cryptic_flamethrower.md) | Blitz | Static check | Base capacity 3 + 2 for selecting both skulls = 5 shared charges. One fire order and one rescue consume 2, leaving 3. Fire is capped at 15 seconds and 10 metres; the extra 2 charges do not apply to Purgator alone. |
| [Medicae Servo-Skull](cryptic_servo_skull_inject_ally.md) | Blitz | Static check | With maximum Toughness 100: 100 × 20% = 20 restored per second; 20 × 5 = 100 at most, capped by maximum Toughness. Toughness Damage taken multiplier 0.25 means 75% reduction. Each rescue spends 1 shared Blitz charge; capacity 3 becomes 5 when both skulls are selected. |
| [Arc Grenades](cryptic_grenade_ability_arc_grenade.md) | Blitz | Static check | Up to 4 initial arcs, each up to 5 jumps within 12m. Direct explosion/jump attack 0; damage is ongoing Electrocution, not a fixed Health loss. Illustrative 12 × 5 = 60 assumes 5 actual ticks. 3 × 0.04 = 0.12 Capacitance charges; at 50 points/charge, 2 per kill and 6 for 3. Grenade uses remain 3 and do not naturally recharge. |
| [Artificer Servo-Skull](cryptic_servo_skull_improved.md) | Blitz | Static check | Permanent shooting damage +25% gives 100 × 1.25 = 125; shooting cooldown ×0.5 gives 3 × 0.5 = 1.5s. Skull hits apply target Damage taken +15% for 5s, one refreshing stack, and add 1 Burn stack up to 8; further hits reset Burn duration. |
| [Kinetic Repulsion](cryptic_force_field_capacitance_restore.md) | Blitz | Static check | Each qualifying absorbed Ranged Attack restores 0.025 Combat Ability charges; 10 give 0.25 and 30 reach the 0.75 per-field cap. At 50 points/charge: 50 × 2.5% = 1.25 per attack and 50 × 75% = 37.5 maximum. Recovery uses attack count, not damage amount, and does not refill Blitz uses. |
| [Noospheric Command](cryptic_servo_skull_improved_tagging.md) | Blitz | Static check | Cooldown ×0.15 leaves 15% (85% reduction; about 6.67× frequency); 3 × 0.15 = 0.45s. Permanent ×0.5 multiplies: 3 × 0.5 × 0.15 = 0.225s. Full 2s boost costs 0.3 = 30% of one Capacitance charge; 0.25 is below the normal threshold. Existing duration progress adjusts refresh cost. +567% is an unused format value in the current English template. |
| [Overcharged Arc Grenades](cryptic_arc_grenades_brittleness.md) | Blitz | Static check | Initial target cap 4 + 2 = 6; chain jump count unchanged. 8 × 2.5% = 20% Brittleness; maximum 16 stacks = 40%, lasting 5s with timer refresh. Armour-only example with 100 pre-armour damage, original 0.5 multiplier and Rending coefficient 1: 50 → 70 at 8 stacks, 90 at 16; crossing multiplier 1 requires another calculation. Not an equal final-damage percentage bonus. |
| [Enhanced Arc Grenades](cryptic_arc_grenades_weapon_malfunction.md) | Blitz | Static check | Weapon Malfunction lasts 12s for supported living targets. A hit at 0s expires at 12s; a new qualifying hit at 8s updates expiry to 8 + 12 = 20s. Ongoing shock cannot refresh it because skip_on_hit_proc is true. Base explosion radius 10m; direct explosion/jump attack 0, ongoing shock attack 600 is a power-distribution value rather than fixed Health loss; 1.1s/0.2s alone does not fix tick count. |
| [Overcharged Refraction Emitter](cryptic_force_field_duration_increase.md) | Blitz | Static check | Duration increases from 8s to 12s, 4s longer. Midpoint 12 ÷ 2 = 6s; full timeline 0/6/12s, each 5m. Interrupted at 4s: activation and finish explosions occur; the 6s midpoint does not. This upgrade does not increase explosion radius or damage profile. |
| [Voltaic Resistance](cryptic_force_field_arcs.md) | Blitz | Static check | clamp(ceil(absorbed Ranged Attacks / 6), 1, 4): 0–6 →1 arc, 7–12 →2, 13–18 →3, 19+ →4. Zero absorption still attempts 1, requiring a living valid target within 12m in front (dot >0.5). Each chain has 12m radius and up to 4 jumps. Separate from the field's 5m explosions; count is not damage amount. |
| [Resurgence](cryptic_coherency_regen_aura_improved.md) | Aura | Static check | Personal +25 Toughness applies only to caster; the 50% minimum normal Coherency regeneration rate applies to caster and chain allies. 10 × 0.5 = 5 points/s; 4s restores 20 with sufficient deficit and no interruption. Regeneration delay, recipient modifiers and maximum remain. 50% is a rate multiplier, not maximum Toughness/s. |
| [Ammunition Deposit](cryptic_ammo_aura.md) | Aura | Static check | Caster-only +25 Toughness; mission-wide human-player Ammo Reserve capacity +15%, with no Coherency check in the accepted source. Current and maximum reserves scale: floor(101 ×1.15) = floor(116.15) =116. Multiple sources retain a single 15% step. Exact display near floating-point integer boundaries remains unobserved. |
| [Foe-Render Creed](cryptic_aura_weapon_improved.md) | Aura | Static check | Caster-only maximum Toughness +25; caster and Coherency allies gain hit-mass Cleave +15% and Rending +7.5%. Mass budget 10 ×1.15 =11.5. Armour-only example with 100 pre-armour damage, original multiplier 0.5 and Rending coefficient 1: 50 →57.5; crossing multiplier 1 needs another conversion. Neither 15% extra enemy count nor a direct 7.5% final-damage bonus. |
| [Chordclaw Strike](cryptic_chordclaw.md) | Ability | Static check | Base 3 Capacitance charges; use costs 1 =50 points, naturally restored at 1 point/s →50s. Default Heavy Attack guaranteed Critical Strike; active Rending +50%, Melee Damage +30%, Stun immunity. Same-stage examples: 100 ×1.3 =130, or 100 ×(1+0.2+0.3) =150. Effect maximum 10s; full charging 0.5s. Rending/Critical Strike and profile power are not fixed Health damage. |
| [Restoration Protocol](cryptic_precision_stance_toughness_suppression.md) | Ability | Static check | Restores 10% of maximum Toughness/s while the stance remains active; with maximum 150 and no other bonuses, 150 ×0.1 =15 points/s and 4s gives 60. Recovery modifiers, limits and current deficit apply. Activation clears Suppression once; switching off does not repeat that clear. |
| [Writ of Ammunition Enumeration](cryptic_precision_stance_fire_rate_increased.md) | Ability | Static check | While the stance is active, ranged Fire Rate starts at +15%; continuously maintaining it for 4s adds 0.30 −0.15 =0.15 for a total of +30%. Reactivation resets the 4s timer. With baseline 5 rounds/s: 5 ×1.15 =5.75, then 5 ×1.30 =6.5; other action and weapon limits apply separately. |
| [Voltaic Arcs](cryptic_discharge_generates_arcs.md) | Ability | Static check | One forward arc per full charge consumed; normal use consumes at most 3 charges →at most 3 arcs, while the template cap is 5. Living, targetable enemy initial targets within 12m and flattened-direction dot >0.5; each chain has 12m radius, one child/step and at most 4 links. Link attack power 0, with Impact and subsequent Electrocution depending on damage profiles and targets; arc count is not fixed Health damage. |
| [Voltaic Motivator](cryptic_discharge_attack_speed_increase.md) | Ability | Static check | Attack Speed bonus =0.05 +0.05n, lasting 15s: 1/2/3 charges →10%/15%/20%. Affected action duration 1s ÷1.20 ≈0.833s for 3 charges. A forced effective count of 3 also uses 3 for scaling. Unused display charge=2 is neither an execution threshold nor a placeholder in the current English. |
| [Voltaic Overcharge](cryptic_discharge_toughness.md) | Ability | Static check | On-use recovery 25% of maximum Toughness per full/effective charge; always_full_charges_bonus uses 3. Qualifying explosion hits restore 1% each, checking living target and profile rather than positive Health damage. Example maximum 100, 2 charges and 5 hits: 100 ×(2 ×25% +5 ×1%) =55 points; only 40 if deficit is 40. Modifiers and recovery limits apply; fractional progress is not a full charge. |
| [Axial Slash](cryptic_chordclaw_horizontal_swipe.md) | Ability | Static check | Horizontal Heavy sweep remains guaranteed crit; holding to charge retains original Heavy stab. Attack power by target: 500/480/450/410/360, then 300; Impact 100/90/80/70/60, then 50. Same-condition damage distribution: second/first 480 ÷500 =96%; if first takes 100, second 96 and fifth 100 ×360 ÷500 =72. Actual targets and damage depend on Cleave, positions, armour and hit location; no new talent charge cost. |
| [Probing Strikes](cryptic_chordclaw_quick_stab_combo.md) | Ability | Static check | Three guaranteed-crit quick stabs; held input retains regular Heavy stab. Each Health-damaging stab adds 6 Bleed stacks: 6 +6 +6 =18 (cap), or two add 12; misses add none. Bleed duration 9.5s, interval 0.375, new stacks restart duration. Existing stacks, 18-stack cap, target, power, armour and modifiers apply; six stacks do not mean six damage. |
