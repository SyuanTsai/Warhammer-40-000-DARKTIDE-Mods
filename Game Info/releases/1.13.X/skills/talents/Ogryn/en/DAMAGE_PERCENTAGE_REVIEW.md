# Ogryn: percentage and calculation review

[繁體中文](../DAMAGE_PERCENTAGE_REVIEW.md) | [Player descriptions](README.md) | [Technical index](SOURCE_INDEX.md)

Release 1.13.1, fixed SHA `7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e`. Entries distinguish Damage from Power, additional critical damage, same-stage addition from separate multiplication, speed from action time, and maximum Toughness from its deficit. Examples are static derivations, not in-game measurements.

| Talent | Quantity and calculation | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| [Bombs Away!](ogryn_box_explodes.md) | Box direct impact, ordinary child blast and fuse/count calculations are separate. Base 6 child grenades; Bigger Box of Hurt adds 3. | Box against Carapace: 1850 × 0.15 = 277.5. Child centre: 10 Unarmoured or 2 Carapace; six Unarmoured central hits total 60. Fuse i: 0.8 + 0.4i to 0.8 + 0.8i seconds. |
| [Frag Bomb](ogryn_grenade_frag.md) | Ordinary blast damage is separate from the instant-kill flag. Radius 16m, close radius 2m, PowerLevel 500. | Close Unarmoured: 1500 × 1 = 1500. Carapace range 0.8–1.25, midpoint 1.025: 1537.5. Fuse 2s without collision; collision resets the timer to use 0.9s, with a 0.6s activation threshold. |
| [Big Friendly Rock](ogryn_grenade_friend_rock.md) | Direct-hit profile 1200; standard PowerLevel 500 gives output multiplier 1. Charge cycle 45s, capacity 4. | Close Unarmoured: 1200 × 1 = 1200; Carapace: 1200 × 0.25 = 300. From empty, 4 × 45 = 180s without modifiers. Specific instant kills and enemy overrides are separate from this example. |
| [That One Didn't Count](ogryn_replenish_rock_on_miss.md) | Restore 1 Blitz charge at projectile completion; 5s cooldown, maximum 4 rocks. | Last-rock refund: 0 → 1. A second qualifying projectile completing at 4s is still inside the 5s cooldown and gives no charge; this compares completion time, not throw-input time. |
| [Bigger Box of Hurt](ogryn_big_box_of_hurt_more_bombs.md) | Add 3 to the box cluster count, whose base is 6. | 6 + 3 = 9 child grenades; this is a count increase, not more box charges or a fixed total-damage multiplier. |
| [Bonebreaker's Aura](ogryn_melee_damage_coherency_improved.md) | melee_damage = 0.1, with same-stage addition; upgraded priority 2 replaces base 0.075 at priority 1. | Alone: 100 × 1.10 = 110. With same-stage +20%: 100 × (1 + 20% + 10%) = 130. Maximum 1 stack; do not add the base 7.5% again. |
| [Coward Culling](ogryn_damage_vs_suppressed_coherency.md) | damage_vs_suppressed = 0.2 for aura recipients; personal Suppression increase = 0.25. | Against a Suppressed target, isolated 100 × 1.20 = 120 damage. Personal Suppression: 10 × 1.25 = 12.5. The two bonuses affect different quantities and recipients. |
| [Stay Close!](ogryn_toughness_regen_aura.md) | toughness_replenish_modifier = 0.2, applied to eligible restoration amounts; ignore-buff paths bypass it. | 15 × 1.20 = 18, capped by the deficit: with only 5 missing, restore 5. Natural Coherency regeneration reads separate rate attributes. |
| [Loyal Protector](ogryn_taunt_shout.md) | Radius 12m, taunted duration 15s; repeats at 3s and 6s refresh the unique buff. | In range at 0/3/6s: last taunt can expire at 6 + 15 = 21s. Repeats use current position and do not Stagger; base cooldown 50s, one charge. |
| [Indomitable](ogryn_longer_charge.md) | Base maximum 12m → 24m, distance display value 1 → 100%. Post-charge movement_speed and melee_attack_speed each 0.25 for 5s. Retained lunging passive damage_taken_multiplier 0.75. | Distance 12 × 2 = 24m; 1s speed-controlled melee action ÷ 1.25 = 0.8s; 5m/s × 1.25 = 6.25m/s; isolated incoming 100 × 0.75 = 75. Impact Health damage is separate from Pulverise Bleed. |
| [Point-Blank Barrage](ogryn_special_ammo.md) | ranged_attack_speed = 0.25, reload_speed = 0.65, Close Range Damage +15%, movement penalty multiplier 0.5, ammo_return = 0.5. Duration 12s, base cooldown 60s. | 1s firing interval ÷ 1.25 = 0.8s; 3s reload ÷ 1.65 ≈ 1.82s. A 40% slowdown becomes 20%, so base 5m/s rises from 3 to 4m/s. Counted 20 rounds × 50% = 10 returned, including saved_ammo in the basis and subject to capacity. |
| [Stomping Boots](ogryn_charge_toughness.md) | Toughness.replenish_percentage = 0.1 of maximum, per qualifying ogryn_lunge on_hit while is_lunging. | Maximum 100 × 10% × 3 hits = 30; with only 20 missing, restore 20. No target-unit deduplication or extra cooldown in this passive; other replenishment bonuses are separate. |
| [Pulverise](ogryn_charge_applies_bleed.md) | Apply 5 stacks once per target per charge; generic Bleed maximum 16, retention 1.5s, tick 0.5s. Power uses smoothstep(n/16). | Isolated Unarmoured Health damage per tick = 87.5 × (n/16)² × (3 − 2n/16): n=5 → ≈20.29, n=8 → 43.75. PowerLevel is an input, not the final Health damage. |
| [Go Again!](ogryn_taunt_staggers_reduce_cooldown.md) | Restore 0.015 of one combat ability charge resource cost per qualifying melee/push Stagger, throttled at 0.1s. | Loyal Protector base 50s × 1.5% = 0.75s per trigger; 10 effective triggers restore 7.5s plus natural recovery, subject to the ability resource cap. |
| [Hail of Fire](ogryn_special_ammo_armor_pen.md) | During the 12s stance, ranged_damage and ranged_rending_multiplier each 0.15. Rending alters the armour modifier; excess above 1 uses coefficient 0.25. | With assumed base 100 and armour modifier 0.5: original 50, modified 100 × 1.15 × (0.5 + 0.15) = 74.75. The damage bonus and Rending are separate; results depend on the weapon profile and armour. |
| [Light 'em Up](ogryn_special_ammo_fire_shots.md) | Up to 4 Burn stacks per shot/target, talent addition cap 16; generic flamer_assault maximum 31 supplies the smoothstep denominator. Retention 4s, tick 0.5s. | Power inputs: 4 stacks ≈22.83, 16 ≈262.09. Isolated Unarmoured Health damage per tick = 600 × (n/31)² × (3 − 2n/31): ≈27.39 and ≈314.51. Without duration extensions, 16 stacks fully decay in about 12s. |
| [Valuable Distraction](ogryn_taunt_damage_taken_increase.md) | Affected enemies have damage_taken_multiplier 1.2 for 15s; repeated waves refresh one stack. | 100 × 1.2 = 120. Together with Soften Them Up at a different stage: 100 × 1.15 × 1.2 = 138. |
| [Bullet Bravado](ogryn_ranged_stance_toughness_regen.md) | During the 12s stance, shooting restores 0.025 of maximum Toughness; reloading restores 0.15. | Maximum Toughness 100: four shooting events and one reload restore 100 × (4 × 2.5% + 15%) = 25; a deficit of 10 caps restoration at 10. |
| [No Pain!](ogryn_taunt_restore_toughness.md) | Each taunt restores 10% immediately; each affected enemy adds 0.5% per second, maximum 20 stacks / 10% per second, for 3.25s. English displays 3s. | Three waves affecting four enemies each: 30 immediate; rates 2, 4, 6 per second. 30 + 2 × 3 + 4 × 3 + 6 × 3.25 = 67.5, with sufficient deficit and no other bonuses. |
| [Trample](ogryn_charge_trample.md) | Each valid charge-hit event adds damage 0.025; maximum 20 stacks / 50%, duration 10s refreshed on further hits. | Four hits: 100 × (1 + 4 × 2.5%) = 110; 20 stacks = 150. With another 20% at the same stage: 170. |
