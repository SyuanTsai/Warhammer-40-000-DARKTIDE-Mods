# Arbites: percentage and calculation review

[繁體中文](../DAMAGE_PERCENTAGE_REVIEW.md) | [Player descriptions](README.md) | [Technical index](SOURCE_INDEX.md)

Release 1.13.1, fixed SHA `7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e`. Entries distinguish the actual quantity and calculation assumptions. Examples are static derivations, not in-game measurements.

| Talent | Quantity and calculation | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| [Remote Detonation](adamant_whistle.md) | Electrocution multiplies the target damage-taken stage by 1.1; armour, blast and recovery calculations are separate. | Isolated damage 100 → 110; with a separate 25% attacker bonus, 100 × 1.25 × 1.1 = 137.5. Central blast: 600 Unarmoured or 600 × 0.5 = 300 Flak damage before other effects. Two charges recover sequentially in 50/100s without other modifiers. |
| [Arbites Grenade](adamant_grenade_improved.md) | Distinguish blast PowerLevel from the central damage-profile baseline, armour modifiers and separate upgrade nodes. | Isolated central damage 1,500 × armour modifier: 1,500 Unarmoured, 750 Flak, 300 Carapace. Capacity 3 → 4; no automatic cooldown refill from this ability alone. |
| [Voltaic Shock Mine](adamant_shock_mine.md) | Separate per-tick armour damage from status duration and the deployed-mine timers. | Isolated tick: 8 × 0.5 = 4 Unarmoured damage; 8 × 1 = 8 Flak/Carapace damage. Random 0.3–0.8s intervals prevent a fixed total. Arming 1s, waiting about 150s, active period 15s and each status 3s are distinct. |
| [Part of the Squad](adamant_companion_coherency.md) | Toughness damage taken; an additional 7.5 percentage points of reduction in the same additive stage, not Health damage reduction. | Isolated 100 × (1 − 0.075) = 92.5 Toughness damage. With an existing same-stage 10% reduction: 100 × (1 − 0.10 − 0.075) = 82.5. |
| [Ruthless Efficiency](adamant_reload_speed_aura.md) | Reload Speed +12.5%; convert speed to duration using 1 / 1.125. | Isolated 2s reload: 2 / 1.125 ≈ 1.78s, approximately an 11.11% duration reduction. Weapon action stages and other modifiers still apply. |
| [Breaking Dissent](adamant_damage_vs_staggered_aura.md) | Additive damage modifier +0.10, conditional on a Staggered target. | Isolated 100 × 1.10 = 110 damage; no Stagger gives 100. With a same-stage 25% bonus: 100 × (1 + 0.25 + 0.10) = 135. Do not multiply every percentage in sequence. |
| [Break the Line](adamant_charge.md) | Post-charge Damage +25% and Impact +50% for 6s; distinguish additive damage from Impact. | Isolated 100 damage → 125; 100 Impact units → 150. Another same-stage 25% damage bonus gives 100 × (1 + 0.25 + 0.25) = 150 damage. |
| [Nuncio-Aquila](adamant_area_buff_drone_improved.md) | Replenishment is 7.5% of maximum Toughness per second; enemy damage taken is a separate ×1.15 stage. | Continuous 2s in range: 7.5% × 2 = 15% maximum Toughness, capped by the deficit. Isolated incoming damage 100 × 1.15 = 115. Leaving range removes the area effects. |
| [Castigator's Stance](adamant_stance.md) | Separate maximum-Toughness replenishment, ×0.3 damage taken, +20% PowerLevel and +15% Movement Speed. | Toughness 40/100 → 100/100 restores 60. Isolated incoming damage 100 → 30; PowerLevel 500 → 600; speed 5 → 5.75m/s. PowerLevel does not guarantee the same percentage change to Damage, Impact and Cleave. |
| [Blessed Armament](adamant_stance_ranged_kills_transfer_ammo.md) | ceil(Clip capacity × 0.10), limited by the Clip deficit and available Reserve. | Capacity 30 → request 3 rounds; if only one is missing, transfer one. No ammunition is created. The once-per-Attack claim remains unconfirmed against timestamp throttling. |
| [Writ of Execution](adamant_stance_elite_kills_stack_damage.md) | Additive damage stat; +7.5% per stack, maximum six stacks. | 6 × 7.5% = 45%. With another same-stage +25%: 100 × (1 + 0.45 + 0.25) = 170 damage. Hit and target defenses still apply. |
| [Bloodlust](adamant_stance_dog_bloodlust.md) | Additive companion-damage stat +75%, excluding Bleed. | Isolated direct damage 100 × (1 + 0.75) = 175. With another same-stage 25%: 200. With Kill Order +50%: 100 × (1 + 0.75 + 0.50) = 225. |
| [Inspiring Recitation](adamant_drone_buff_talent.md) | Toughness damage multiplier 0.7; +30% Revive Speed; +10% Attack Speed. | Isolated Toughness damage 100 × 0.7 = 70. Revive duration 5 ÷ 1.3 ≈ 3.85s; adjustable attack duration 1 ÷ 1.1 ≈ 0.91s. Speed increases are not equal percentage reductions in duration. |
| [Fear of Justice](adamant_drone_debuff_talent.md) | Melee-damage and melee attack-speed additive modifiers −0.25; isolated speed multiplier 0.75. | Isolated melee damage 100 × (1 − 0.25) = 75. Adjustable action duration 1 ÷ 0.75 ≈ 1.33s (+33.3%), excluding minimum-duration limits and other modifiers. The English display says +50% interval; that would be 1.5s. |
| [Commendation from Condemnation](adamant_charge_toughness.md) | Recovery fractions of maximum resources: min(0.20 × n, 1.00) Toughness and min(0.15 × n, 0.75) Stamina. | n is the distinct eligible target count in one charge. Three targets give 60% / 45%; five or more give at most 100% / 75%. Actual restoration is capped by each current deficit. |
| [Targeted Brutality](adamant_charge_cooldown_reduction.md) | Cooldown restoration in seconds, not a damage percentage. | min(0.5 × ordinary hits + 1 × Elite/Specialist/Monstrosity hits, 5s), further limited by remaining cooldown. Six ordinary and three higher-category hits total 6s, capped at 5s. Base cooldown is 20s. |
| [Engage](adamant_charge_longer_distance.md) | Distance addition in metres. | 3.75m base + 3.75m stat increase = 7.5m target distance. The display is the resulting total, not the added distance. Actual displacement can be shortened by collision, path or cancellation. |
| [Kill Order](adamant_dog_damage_after_ability.md) | Additive companion-damage stat +50%, excluding Bleed. | Isolated non-Bleed damage 100 × (1 + 0.50) = 150. With Bloodlust +75% in the same stage: 100 × (1 + 0.50 + 0.75) = 225. The 12s buff refreshes without self-stacking. |
| [Execution Order](adamant_execution_order.md) | Additive player damage +10%; companion damage +150%, excluding Bleed; +10% Attack Speed; 15% maximum-Toughness recovery. | Isolated player damage 100→110, companion damage 100→250. With another same-stage 25% player bonus: 100 × (1 + 0.25 + 0.10) = 135. Adjustable action 1 ÷ 1.1 ≈ 0.91s. Maximum Toughness 100 gives 15 points, capped by the deficit. |
| [Terminus Warrant](adamant_terminus_warrant.md) | Melee or ranged PowerLevel +10%; melee Toughness-damage multiplier 0.8; ranged Suppression and Cleave +50%. | Isolated PowerLevel 500→550; with an existing same-stage 20% bonus, 600→650. PowerLevel is not final damage. Melee Toughness damage 100→80 while the buff is active. Core magnitude is fixed for one or 20 stacks. |
| [Forceful](adamant_forceful.md) | Per-stack Impact +5% (additive); damage-taken multiplier 0.975 (multiplicative). | Ten stacks give Impact 10 × 0.05 = +0.50 and damage multiplier 0.975^10 ≈ 0.77633: damage 100→77.63, about 22.4% reduction, not 25%. This isolates Forceful; other modifiers still apply. |
| [Lone Wolf](adamant_disable_companion.md) | Damage +20%; Attack Speed +10%; Toughness-damage modifier −0.15. | Isolated damage 100→120; same-stage +25% gives 145. Adjustable action 1 ÷ 1.1 ≈ 0.91s. Toughness damage 100→85; another same-stage −0.10 gives 75. The defensive modifier applies to Toughness. |
| [Will of the Lex](adamant_forceful_toughness_regen_per_stack.md) | Recovery rate 0.005 of maximum Toughness per second per Forceful stack. | Maximum Toughness 100 with 10 stacks gives 100 × 0.005 × 10 = 5 points/s before recovery modifiers. If only 3 points are missing, replenish at most 3. This percentage uses maximum Toughness, not the deficit. |
| [Adamant Will](adamant_forceful_stun_immune_and_block_all.md) | Stun/Slowdown Immunity and conditional blocking capability; no percentage-damage calculation. | Activates at 10 Forceful stacks; leaving maximum stacks grants a 3s linger. Normally unblockable attacks require a perfect-block state and the shared blocking checks; this does not imply continuous invulnerability. |
| [Targets Acquired](adamant_forceful_offensive.md) | Attack Speed +10%; damaging Cleave hit-mass budget +50%. | With this effect alone: a scalable 1s action takes 1 ÷ 1.1 ≈ 0.91s, and a Cleave budget of 10 becomes 15. The Cleave percentage does not guarantee a fixed number of additional enemies or increase stagger Cleave. |
| [Arbites Vigilant](adamant_forceful_ability_damage.md) | Strength (PowerLevel) +2.5% per converted stack, up to +25% at 10 stacks. | Five stacks: +12.5%; six: +15%; ten: +25%. With this effect alone, 500 PowerLevel becomes 625 at 10 stacks. This is a PowerLevel increase; final Damage, Impact and Cleave still use their own formulas. |
| [Judicial Force](adamant_forceful_stagger_on_low_high.md) | Threshold-triggered stagger explosion; no percentage bonus. | Radius 2.5m; explosion creation uses PowerLevel 750 and the ogryn_charge_finish damage profile. Actual Damage and Stagger depend on the profile, target and shared explosion calculations. Separate 5s cooldowns govern reaching zero and maximum stacks. |
| [Obstinate](adamant_terminus_warrant_cdr.md) | Ability Cooldown Regeneration +33%; additional 0.33s resource restoration per second. | Nominal 12-tick total: 12 × 0.33 = 3.96s. Starting with 20s remaining: 20 − 12 − 3.96 = 4.04s after 12s and 12 ticks. This is a regeneration-rate bonus, with actual ticks and resource limits applying. |
| [Terminal Decree](adamant_terminus_warrant_support.md) | Toughness recovery: 1% of maximum Toughness per spent stack, calculated for each recipient. | At 100 maximum Toughness, 20 stacks restore 20 points and seven restore 7 points. A recipient missing only 8 points receives at most 8. Recovery modifiers and recovery blocking also apply. |
| [Efficient Killer](adamant_execution_order_crit.md) | Critical Hit Chance +10 percentage points; additional critical damage +25%. | Chance: 5% → 15%. For normal/critical damage 100/150: 100 + (150 − 100) × 1.25 = 162.5, a whole-hit gain of about 8.33%. With normal/critical damage 100/200, the result is 225, a 12.5% whole-hit gain. |
| [Malocator](adamant_execution_order_cdr.md) | Cooldown Regeneration +50%; additional 0.5s resource restoration per second. | Nominal eight-tick total: 8 × 0.5 = 4s. Starting with 20s remaining: 20 − 8 − 4 = 8s after 8s and eight ticks. This is a regeneration-rate bonus, with actual ticks and resource limits applying. |
| [No Lenience](adamant_execution_order_rending.md) | Rending +10%; armor-damage modifier, with shared cap 1. | At base damage 100 and armor coefficient 0.5, damage rises from 50 to 100 × (0.5 + 0.1) = 60 (+20%). If the coefficient is already 1 and the armor permits excess conversion: 100 × (1 + 0.1 × 0.25) = 102.5. |
| [Keeping Protocol](adamant_execution_order_permastack.md) | Damage vs Monstrosities +1% per stack, additive; damage taken multiplier 0.99 per stack. | At 30 stacks: +30% outgoing Damage and 0.99^30 ≈ 0.7397 incoming multiplier (~26.0% reduction). With this effect alone, outgoing 100 becomes 130; incoming 100 becomes ~73.97. |
| [Not Far Behind](adamant_pinning_dog_bonus_moving_towards.md) | Damage +10%, additive in the shared damage stage; Movement Speed +10%. | With this effect alone, base damage 100 becomes 110. With another +25% in the same stage, it becomes 135. Base movement 5m/s becomes 5 × 1.1 = 5.5m/s. Retriggering refreshes duration, not magnitude. |
| [Go Get 'Em!](adamant_companion_focus_ranged.md) | Cyber-Mastiff Damage against Ranged Enemies +50%, additive in the shared damage stage. | With this effect alone, base damage 100 becomes 150. With another +25% in the same stage: 100 × (1 + 25% + 50%) = 175. Other target types do not receive this specific bonus. |
