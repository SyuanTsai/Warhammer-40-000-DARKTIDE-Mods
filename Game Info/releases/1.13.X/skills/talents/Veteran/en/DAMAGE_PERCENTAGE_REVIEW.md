# Veteran: percentage and calculation review

[繁體中文](../DAMAGE_PERCENTAGE_REVIEW.md) | [Player descriptions](README.md) | [Technical index](SOURCE_INDEX.md)

Source: Release 1.13.1, fixed SHA `7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e`. Review entries below identify the actual quantity and example assumptions. They do not inherit the Chinese review's counts or certify talents absent from this table. Examples are static derivation, not in-game measurements.

| Talent | Quantity and calculation | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| [Demolition Stockpile](veteran_replenish_grenades.md) | One grenade per interval; fixed-loadout deficits restore sequentially: `60 × 2 = 120 seconds` for Frag/Smoke, `90 × 2 = 180 seconds` for Krak. Update boundaries make timing approximate. | No damage percentage is claimed. Capacity and one-grant amount are separate; restoration-amount bonuses are bypassed, not capacity limits. |
| [Precision Strikes](veteran_increased_weakspot_damage.md) | Add `0.30` to the weakspot finesse multiplier. At the isolated stage, before `B + F × (1 + s)`, after `B + F × (1 + s + 0.30)`, gain `0.30F / [B + F × (1 + s)]` when before-damage is positive. | Examples give 8.57%, 15%, 20% and 13.33% whole-hit increases under their stated assumptions. The 30% stat is not a universal whole-hit increase; an existing 25% component bonus becomes 55%, giving a 24% relative component increase. |
| [Exhilarating Takedown](veteran_replenish_toughness_on_weakspot_kill.md) | Recovery is `maximum Toughness × 0.15 × recovery modifiers`, limited by the deficit and permissions. Effective stack factor is `0.9ⁿ`, `n ≤ 3`; reduction is `1 − 0.9ⁿ`. | Three stacks reduce Toughness damage by 27.1%, not 30%. An independent `0.8` Toughness multiplier combines as `0.8 × 0.9³ = 0.5832`, a total 41.68% reduction at that stage. Do not apply these percentages to all health damage. |
| [Marksman's Focus](veteran_snipers_focus.md) | Finesse contribution is `0.075nF`; whole-hit gain `0.075nF / [B + F × (1 + s)]` for positive baseline. Affected action time is `T₀ / (1 + r + 0.01n)` with other scale factors 1 and no limit binding. | Ten stacks add 75 percentage points to the extra-component modifier, not 75% to every hit. Examples give 21.43% and 37.5% whole-hit gains; a four-second action becomes about 3.64 seconds, a 9.09% time saving from +10% speed. Effective caps are 10 or optionally 15; raw cap 31 adds no further stats. |
| [Overwatch](veteran_combat_ability_extra_charge.md) | Add one stored charge and `0.33` to the cost multiplier. Isolated Infiltrate threshold `40 × 1.33 = 53.2 resource units`; rate `1 unit/s`; time `53.2s`, full two-charge pool `106.4s`. | Cooldown cost, regen speed and actual consumption are distinct. Stored uses refill sequentially; existing same-stage cost modifiers combine additively. With a −20% cost delta, 32s becomes 45.2s, a 41.25% increase relative to that modified baseline. |
| [Long Range Assassin](veteran_snipers_focus_increased_stacks.md) | Raise the effective cap to 15 while retaining `0.075` ranged finesse and `0.01` reload speed per stack. At 15 stacks: deltas `1.125 / 0.15`. | With `B = 100`, `F = 40`, no other modifiers, damage is 140 at zero, 170 at ten and 185 at fifteen stacks. Gain is 32.14% relative to zero, but 8.82% relative to the old cap. An affected 4s action takes about 3.48s, saving 13.04% time. |
| [Chink in their Armour](veteran_snipers_focus_rending_bonus.md) | Add `0.15` Rending at 10 or more raw Focus stacks; combine sources before the shared cap. | Isolated Carapace examples with pre-armor damage 100: original armor multiplier 0.5 gives 50 → 65 (+30%); 0.8 gives 80 → 95 (+18.75%); original 0.5 plus existing Rending 0.1 gives 60 → 75 (+25%). These exclude critical/weakspot finesse and other effects. |
| [Tunnel Vision](veteran_snipers_focus_toughness_bonus.md) | Add `0.04` Toughness replenishment per effective Focus stack; qualifying ranged weakspot kills request `0.10 × maximum Stamina`. | With no other recovery modifiers and enough missing Toughness, a 20-point event becomes 24 points at five stacks, or 20.8 at one. Maximum Stamina 6 requests 0.6 units; a deficit of 0.2 limits the return to 0.2. This is resource recovery, not direct damage gain. |
| [Infiltrate](veteran_invisibility_on_combat_ability.md) | Single-use `damage = 0.3` bonus is active during Stealth and for 8 seconds after exit; base cost 40 and natural recovery 1 unit/s. | With no other damage bonuses, overlapping uses or later modifiers, a 100-damage baseline becomes 130. Toughness 30/100 recovers 70 points. Exit at about 3s leaves the damage bonus until about 11s, with about 29s remaining on the unmodified cooldown. |
| [Low Profile](veteran_reduced_threat_after_combat_ability.md) | Multiply the affected enemy target-selection weight by `0.1`; the single active instance retains its first ten-second deadline after Stealth exit. | Isolated weight 100 becomes 10, not a 10% attack chance. Exit at about 3s gives a deadline near 13s; another grant at 7s during the same countdown leaves about 6s instead of restarting it. |
| [Tactical Awareness](veteran_elite_kills_reduce_cooldown.md) | Specialist kills grant a three-second, single-instance extra one-unit/s recovery effect; duration refresh keeps the tick cadence. | With base natural recovery 1 unit/s and enough missing resource, 25 seconds remaining becomes `25 − 3 natural − 3 extra = 19s` after about 3s. This is resource recovery over time, not an instant six-second reduction or a damage bonus. |
| [Voice of Command](veteran_combat_ability_stagger_nearby_enemies.md) | Clear the user's Toughness deficit; unmodified charge cost 40 regenerates at 1 unit/s. | Toughness 30/100 restores 70 points to return to 100. One fully spent charge takes `40 / 1 = 40s`. These are resource/recovery examples; the 2.5s heavy fallback parameter is not a universal stagger-duration guarantee. |
| [Only In Death Does Duty End](veteran_combat_ability_revive_nearby_allies.md) | Revive special rule; same-named cost/radius buff is not installed by this node. | Unmodified radius remains 9m and one spent use recharges in `40 / 1 = 40s`. Knocked Down allies at 5m and 8m can both be revived; one at 10m is outside. This is a revive/resource effect, not a damage bonus. |
| [Marksman](veteran_increased_weakspot_power_after_combat_ability.md) | Add `0.20` at the weakspot PowerLevel stage; this is separate from the extra finesse component. | Synthetic default-curve power 500 → 600 gives base damage 100 → 120 and impact 5 → 6 under the stated assumptions. Existing same-stage 25% power changes 125 → 145 (+16% relative). This does not guarantee 20% final damage, 20% longer stagger or extra cleave. |
| [Duty and Honour](veteran_combat_ability_increase_and_restore_toughness_to_coherency.md) | Add 75 flat maximum Toughness per independent ten-second instance, after percentage modifiers; current Toughness also rises by 75. | Ally 30/100 → 105/175; caster → 175/175 through personal recovery. On expiry, 160/175 → 100/100 or 60/175 → 60/100. Two active grants on a 100-point maximum give 250; base 100 with +20% gives 195 with one grant. |
| [Close Quarters Killzone](veteran_increased_close_damage_after_combat_ability.md) | Add `0.15 × (1 − sqrt(clamp((d − 12.5) / 17.5, 0, 1)))` to the general damage multiplier. | Isolated 100 damage becomes 115 within 12.5m, 107.5 at 16.875m, and 100 at 30m or farther. An existing same-stage 25% bonus changes 125 → 140 (+12% relative), rather than multiplying 125 by 1.15. |
| [Hunter's Resolve](veteran_toughness_bonus_leaving_invisibility.md) | Active Toughness-damage factors multiply: one instance `0.5`, two `0.5² = 0.25`. Each instance begins its own 10s exit countdown. | Isolated 100 Toughness damage becomes 50 or, with two active instances, 25 (75% total reduction). Eight seconds in Stealth gives about 18s total coverage; exit at 2s gives about 12s. This stat does not improve health damage taken or maximum Toughness. |
