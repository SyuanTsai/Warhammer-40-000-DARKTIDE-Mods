# Veteran: percentage and calculation review

[繁體中文](../DAMAGE_PERCENTAGE_REVIEW.md) | [Player descriptions](README.md) | [Technical index](SOURCE_INDEX.md)

Source: Release 1.13.1, fixed SHA `7e662fcda16219d775b84af50322be2e9cd9d62e`. Review entries below identify the actual quantity and example assumptions. They do not inherit the Chinese review's counts or certify talents absent from this table. Examples are static derivation, not in-game measurements.

| Talent | Quantity and calculation | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| [Smoke Grenade](veteran_smoke_grenade.md) | No base damage percentage is described. Separate base fuse, cloud duration, charge capacity and concealment linger. | Base timeline 1.5 + 15 = 16.5s; Grenade Tinkerer cloud 15 × 2 = 30s and timeline 31.5s. One throw: 3 → 2 charges. The 10m detonation blast is not the 4.5m sight-blocking core. |
| [Grenadier](veteran_extra_grenade.md) | Add one to grenade capacity and give each throw a 20% additional-projectile chance without another charge consumption. | Base 3 → 4 capacity. Successful extra throw: four charges → three and two projectiles. Ten throws with replenishment give two expected extras, not a guaranteed two or fixed damage increase. |
| [Grenade Tinkerer](veteran_improved_grenades.md) | Frag damage/radius +0.25, Krak damage +0.75, Smoke duration +1; radius/duration are additive over baseline one. | Isolated damage stages: 500 → 625 Frag and 2,400 → 4,200 Krak. Frag radii 2/10m → 2.5/12.5m; Smoke 15s → 30s. Bleed does not receive the Frag explosion stat. The unused radius display field is not an English claim. |
| [Krak Grenade](veteran_krak_grenade.md) | The raw text claims devastating damage without a fixed percentage. Separate power normalization, armor multiplier, boss power and outer-blast falloff. | Conditional close/non-boss ordinary hit: 2,400 × 1 = 2,400 unarmoured; 2,400 × 2 = 4,800 Carapace. Collision 0.4s + fuse 1s ≈ 1.4s; no collision 2 + 1 ≈ 3s. These are not universal final damage or timing values. |
| [Demolition Stockpile](veteran_replenish_grenades.md) | One grenade per interval; fixed-loadout deficits restore sequentially: `60 × 2 = 120 seconds` for Frag/Smoke, `90 × 2 = 180 seconds` for Krak. Update boundaries make timing approximate. | No damage percentage is claimed. Capacity and one-grant amount are separate; restoration-amount bonuses are bypassed, not capacity limits. |
| [Fully Loaded](veteran_ammo_increase.md) | +0.25 adds to the reserve-capacity multiplier based at 1; floor and hard clamp apply. | Base maximum 100 → 125 rounds; 150 → 187. With existing +20% at base 100, 120 → 145 rounds, about 20.83% relative gain. Magazine capacity is separate. |
| [Lock and Load](veteran_clip_size.md) | +25% changes magazine capacity; per-field flooring and the hard limit apply. Reserve capacity is separate. | 40 → 50 rounds; 7 × 1.25 = 8.75 → 8 rounds. The English rounded up clause is an explicit contradiction. |
| [Tactical Reload](veteran_faster_reload_on_non_empty_clips.md) | +25% reload speed with a non-empty magazine at reload start; retained through that reload. | Affected action 4s ÷ 1.25 = 3.2s: 0.8s or 20% less time. An empty-start action with its own 4s baseline remains 4s from this talent alone; no universal complete reload time is claimed. |
| [Volley Adept](veteran_reload_speed_on_elite_kill.md) | +30% reload speed on an Elite or Specialist kill; next-reload use with no banked extra uses. | Affected action 4s ÷ 1.30 ≈ 3.08s. With Tactical Reload active from a non-empty start: 4s ÷ 1.55 ≈ 2.58s; no universal complete reload time is claimed. |
| [Rending Strikes](veteran_rending_bonus.md) | 10% Rending; armor-dependent adjustment rather than a fixed whole-hit multiplier. | Isolated noncritical, non-weakspot hit: 100 before armor, Carapace, no existing Rending. Armor modifier 0.50 gives 50→60 damage (+20%); modifier 1 gives 100→102.5 (+2.5%). Unarmoured adjustment is zero. |
