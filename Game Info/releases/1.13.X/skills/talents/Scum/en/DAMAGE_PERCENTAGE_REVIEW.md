# Scum: percentage and example review

[繁體中文](../DAMAGE_PERCENTAGE_REVIEW.md) | [Player descriptions](README.md) | [Technical index](SOURCE_INDEX.md)

The review covers Power, general Damage and additional Weakspot/Critical Damage, armour, speed and reciprocal duration, maximum and missing Toughness, and cooldown replenishment multipliers. Calculations use fixed assumptions and existing source derivations; no in-game test was performed.

| Talent | Category | Review | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Blackout](broker_blitz_flash_grenade_improved.md) | Blitz | Static check | With 2 grenades and 19 kills, the next eligible kill gives 3 grenades and resets progress. Extra Pouches: 5 + 1 = 6 capacity. |
| [Boom Bringer](broker_blitz_missile_launcher.md) | Blitz | Static check | Unmodified inner explosion without falloff: 20 × (500 × 2800 ÷ 10000) = 2800. Unarmoured ×1.25 → 3500; Carapace ×2.4 → 6720; excludes direct impact, hit-location modifiers, enemy reduction and other talents. |
| [Chem Grenade](broker_blitz_tox_grenade.md) | Blitz | Static check | At 6 stacks, each Toxin calculation has input Power 500 × 6 ÷ 30 = 100, before the Toxin curve and armour. Other sources can raise the same Toxin above 6; the area limit is not a universal maximum. |
| [Gunslinger Improved](broker_aura_gunslinger_improved.md) | Aura | Static check | Small Ammo sharing = reserve maximum × 15% × 10%, rounded up: 200 → 3 rounds; 100 → ⌈1.5⌉ = 2. Large pickup at reserve 200: 200 × 50% × 10% = 10 rounds. Normal deployed crate at reserve 200 and magazine 30: (200 + 30) × 10% = 23, capped by the missing amount. |
| [Ruffian](broker_coherency_melee_damage.md) | Aura | Static check | Base Melee Damage 100 → 110. With another 25% in the same stage: 100 × (1 + 25% + 10%) = 135. |
| [Anarchist](broker_coherency_anarchist.md) | Aura | Static check | Initial 10% → 10% + 5% = 15%; initial 25% → 30%. Critical Chance adds in percentage points; duplicate copies of this Aura do not apply. |
| [Enhanced Desperado](broker_ability_focus_improved.md) | Ability | Static check | Sprint Speed +20%: 5 × 1.2 = 6 m/s; normalized base 100 × 1.2 = 120. Extension per kill after 20 s: 1 ÷ 5 = 0.2 s; after 40 s: 1 ÷ 5² = 0.04 s. Natural 45-second replenishment starts after the state ends. |
| [Stimm Supply](broker_ability_stimm_field.md) | Ability | Static check | Every 0.25 s, remove at most 0.5 Corruption: 20 ÷ 0.25 × 0.5 = 40 displayed maximum. Actual healing is limited by removable Corruption; cannot clear a fully consumed Health segment. Natural 60-second cooldown resumes after field operation ends. |
| [Rampage!](broker_ability_punk_rage.md) | Ability | Static check | Power 500 × 1.35 = 675 before curves; a 1 s attack at +20% speed takes 1 ÷ 1.20 ≈ 0.83 s. Damage taken: 100 × 0.75 = 75. Per-hit extension after 20 s: 0.3 ÷ 2 = 0.15 s; after 40 s: 0.3 ÷ 2² = 0.075 s. Power is not a fixed final-Damage percentage. |
| [Pulverising Strikes](broker_ability_punk_rage_sub_2.md) | Ability | Static check | Damage and Stagger Cleave capacities: 10 × 1.5 = 15; actual enemy count depends on mass and weapon limits. 10 × 2.5% = 25%; with rage 35%, total modifier 60%, so Power 500 × 1.6 = 800 before curves, not guaranteed +60% final Damage. |
| [Channelled Aggression](broker_ability_punk_rage_sub_1.md) | Ability | Static check | If armour fully accepts Rending: armour multiplier 0.5 + 0.25 = 0.75; pre-armour Damage 100 changes from 50 to 75. Final increase depends on armour and weapon penetration. Light and Ranged Attacks are ineligible; the 0.5 progress setting does not time-gate the effect. |
| [Forge's Bellow](broker_ability_punk_rage_sub_3.md) | Ability | Static check | Enemy Melee Attack Speed: 1 − 0.5 = 0.5; initial 1 s interval → 1 ÷ 0.5 = 2 s, a 100% increase. The debuff lasts 5 s and does not modify Ranged Attack Speed. English's explicit +50% interval increase conflicts with this isolated derivation. |
| [Boiling Blood](broker_ability_punk_rage_sub_4.md) | Ability | Static check | Special hits: 1 s before 30 s; 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5 s after 30 s; 1 ÷ 2² = 0.25 s after 60 s. Ordinary hits keep 0.3 s, falling to 0.3 ÷ 2 = 0.15 s after 20 s. Thirty seconds is a special-hit diminution threshold, not a total state-duration cap. |
| [Focused Resolve](broker_ability_focus_sub_3.md) | Ability | Static check | Per-state restoration cap: 10 × 0.5 = 5 seconds or 5 × 1 = 5 seconds; mixed kills share that cap. With 45 base resource and 1/s natural recovery, restoring 5 leaves 45 − 5 = 40 seconds after focus ends; natural recovery pauses during focus. |
| [Pick Your Targets](broker_ability_focus_sub_2.md) | Ability | Static check | Five stacks give 5 × 3% = 15% additive Ranged Damage: 100 × (1 + 5 × 0.03) = 115 with only this effect. The separate +15% Rending affects armour penetration; it is not another ×1.15 Damage factor or 30% direct Damage. |
| [Practiced Deployment](broker_ability_stimm_field_sub_3.md) | Ability | Static check | A trigger fills a missing charge-cost fraction, including partial recovery during the 60-second cooldown. If 40 seconds remain, restoration makes the ability available within approximately 0.5 seconds; the maximum stays at 1 charge. |
| [Fast Acting Stimms](broker_ability_stimm_field_sub_1.md) | Ability | Static check | Field time 5 s + natural recovery 60 s = approximately 65 s from deployment to a full charge. Effects can linger for 15 s after leaving or field expiry; that time does not extend the resource-regeneration pause. |
| [Booby Trap](broker_ability_stimm_field_sub_2.md) | Ability | Static check | Within 0.5 m, isolated Unarmoured explosion Damage: 20 × 500 × 300 ÷ 10000 = 300; armour multiplier 0.25 gives 300 × 0.25 = 75. Seven stacks are added on positive explosion Damage; 2.6 s Toxin duration and 0.35 s interval do not mean seven direct Damage ticks. Subsequent Toxin Damage is separate. |
| [Nimble](broker_passive_improved_dodges.md) | Keystone | Static check | Isolated Dodge speed: 4 × 1.25 = 5 m/s; an unchanged path takes approximately 1 ÷ 1.25 = 0.8 times the original time. Melee/grab window: 0.25 + 0.15 = 0.40 s; Ranged window: 0 + 0.15 = 0.15 s. |
| [Adrenaline Frenzy](broker_keystone_adrenaline_junkie.md) | Keystone | Static check | Critical Melee hit: 1 + 1 = 2 stacks. Isolated speed: 1 + 0.10 = 1.10; 1 s action → 1 ÷ 1.10 ≈ 0.91 s. Isolated Damage: 100 × (1 + 0.25) = 125; with same-stage +20%: 100 × (1 + 0.20 + 0.25) = 145. |
| [Vulture's Mark](broker_keystone_vultures_mark_on_kill.md) | Keystone | Static check | 3-stack Ranged Damage: 100 × (1 + 3 × 0.05) = 115; with same-stage +20%: 100 × (1 + 0.20 + 0.15) = 135. Base Critical Chance 10% + 3 × 5 percentage points = 25%; maximum Toughness 100 × 0.15 = 15 restored when at least that much is missing. |
| [Chemical Dependency](broker_keystone_chemical_dependency.md) | Keystone | Static check | Three stacks: 1 + 0.10 + 0.10 + 0.10 = 1.30 recovery rate; uninterrupted isolated 60 s recovery → 60 ÷ 1.30 ≈ 46.15 s. After the last use, one stack expires at 90 s; without another gain, the next expires a further 90 s later. |
| [Chem Enhanced](broker_keystone_chemical_dependency_sub_1.md) | Keystone | Static check | 1/2/3 stacks add 5/10/15 percentage points. Scum base 10% + 3 × 5 points = 25% before weapon and other modifiers. Chance adds generic, attack-specific and weapon modifiers, then clamps to 0%–100%; Critical Damage is unchanged. |
| [Chem Fortified](broker_keystone_chemical_dependency_sub_2.md) | Keystone | Static check | Maximum Toughness 100 × 0.50 = 50 restored when allowed by the deficit; current 40 permits 50, while current 80 permits only 20. Three stacks: 0.95³ = 0.857375; incoming Toughness Damage 100 → 85.74, approximately 14.26 less. Health Damage is not directly reduced. |
| [Maxed Out Chems](broker_keystone_chemical_dependency_sub_3.md) | Keystone | Static check | 4-stack rate: 1 + 4 × 0.10 = 1.40; uninterrupted isolated 60 s recovery → 60 ÷ 1.40 ≈ 42.86 s. Duration: 60 − 90 = −30 s; the final cap is 4. Without another gain, one stack decays every 60 s and each removal resets the shared timer. |
| [Vulture's Push](broker_keystone_vultures_mark_aoe_stagger.md) | Keystone | Static check | The explosion centre is 0.65 m above the player, independent of the kill location; radius 3 m. Isolated Super Armour Impact: 0.55 × 0.1 = 0.055 before other Power/Stagger modifiers; attack Power distribution is 0, so no direct Damage. |
| [Patient Hunter](broker_keystone_vultures_mark_increased_duration.md) | Keystone | Static check | Duration: 8 + (12 − 8) = 12 s. Gains at 0 s and 6 s give shared expiry at 18 s without further gains. At 3 stacks, the fourth qualifying kill refreshes 12 s without creating a fourth stack; Vulture's Dodge's separate 1 s duration is unchanged. |
| [Vulture's Dodge](broker_keystone_vultures_mark_dodge_on_ranged_crit.md) | Keystone | Static check | Triggered at 0 s, then refreshed at 0.6 s: one 1 s buff lasts until 1.6 s, without multiple stacks. The supported checking branches are melee, incapacitating_grab and ranged; the buff changes Dodging checks rather than movement or universal Damage immunity. |
| [Adrenaline Assassin](broker_keystone_adrenaline_junkie_sub_1.md) | Keystone | Static check | Non-Weakspot/non-Critical: 0; non-Weakspot/Critical: 0 + 1 = 1; Weakspot/non-Critical: 1 + 2 = 3; Weakspot/Critical: 1 + 2 + 1 = 4. Only Melee hits qualify, and actual gains remain capped at 30 stacks; Frenzy effects retain the core values. |
| [Stoked Rage](broker_keystone_adrenaline_junkie_sub_3.md) | Keystone | Static check | Duration: 10 + (20 − 10) = 20 s. Retriggering at 18 s restarts a full 20 s timer on one buff. The upgrade leaves the core 2 s Adrenaline timer, 30-stack threshold, +10% Melee Attack Speed and +25% Melee Damage unchanged. |
| [Quick and Deadly](broker_passive_close_range_damage_on_dodge.md) | Talent | Static check | **Distance example**: the bonus falls off beyond 12.5 m and reaches zero at 30 m. At 16.875 m, √((16.875 − 12.5) ÷ 17.5) = 0.5; the bonus is 15% × (1 − 0.5) = 7.5%, so base Damage of 100 becomes 107.5. **Close-range example**: this effect alone changes base Damage of 100 to 115; with an existing 25% bonus at the same stage, 100 × (1 + 25% + 15%) = 140. |
| [A Tertium Welcome](broker_passive_first_target_damage.md) | Talent | Static check | **Damage example**: for base Damage of 100 to the first target, this effect alone gives 100 × 1.15 = 115. With an existing 25% Damage bonus at the same stage, 100 × (1 + 25% + 15%) = 140. |
| [In Your Face](broker_passive_close_ranged_damage.md) | Talent | Static check | **Damage example**: at 16.875 m, √((16.875 − 12.5) ÷ 17.5) = 0.5; the bonus is 25% + (10% − 25%) × 0.5 = 17.5%. Base Damage of 100 becomes 117.5. With an existing 25% bonus at the same stage, 100 × (1 + 25% + 17.5%) = 142.5. |
| [Precision Violence](broker_passive_restore_toughness_on_weakspot_kill.md) | Talent | Static check | **Recovery example**: at 100 maximum Toughness, an ordinary hit followed by a Weakspot hit restores 4 then 8 points, totalling 12; a subsequent Critical Weakspot hit in the same attack can restore another 12. Each recovery is capped by the deficit: if only 5 points are missing, it restores at most 5. |
| [Voice of Tertium](broker_passive_restore_toughness_on_close_ranged_kill.md) | Talent | Static check | **Recovery example**: with 100 maximum Toughness, an ordinary Enemy restores 8 points, and an Elite or Specialist restores 15. At 95 current Toughness, either amount is capped at the 5-point deficit. |
| [Float Like a Butterfly](broker_passive_ninja_grants_crit_chance.md) | Talent | Static check | **Chance examples**: an initial 10% becomes 10% + 20% = 30%; an initial 25% becomes 45%. These assume no modifiers such as conversion of Critical strikes into Damage. |
| [Speedloader](broker_passive_reload_speed_on_close_kill.md) | Talent | Static check | **Timing example**: an accelerable reload action normally taking 2 seconds becomes 2 ÷ 1.3 ≈ 1.54 seconds with this effect alone. With an existing 20% bonus at the same stage, 2 ÷ (1 + 20% + 30%) ≈ 1.33 seconds. |
| [Burst of Energy](broker_passive_stun_immunity_on_toughness_broken.md) | Talent | Static check | **Recovery and cooldown example**: at 100 maximum Toughness, this talent alone restores 0 to 50. After triggering, 6 seconds of Stun Immunity precede the 10-second cooldown. With no other changes, eligible triggers are at least 6 + 10 = 16 seconds apart. |
| [Toughness Boost](base_toughness_node_buff_medium_1.md) | Talent | Static check | **Examples**: an initial 100 Toughness becomes 125. With an additional 20% maximum-Toughness bonus, (100 + 25) × 1.2 = 150. |
| [Regained Posture](broker_passive_stamina_on_successful_dodge.md) | Talent | Static check | **Recovery example**: with 5 maximum Stamina, 5 × 10% = 0.5 points per trigger. At 4.8 current Stamina, actual recovery is only 0.2 points. |
| [Slippery Customer](broker_passive_dodge_melee_on_slide.md) | Talent | Static check | While sliding, Melee attacks follow the Dodge checks; this is not direct immunity to all Damage. Starting a slide is not itself a Successful Dodge. Actual avoidance of a qualifying attack is required to trigger Successful-Dodge talents. |
| [Tis but a Scratch](broker_passive_replenish_toughness_on_ranged_toughness_damage.md) | Talent | Static check | **Recovery example**: at 100 maximum Toughness, 10 points per second for a full 3 seconds gives 30 points. Retriggering at 2 seconds can extend recovery to 5 seconds, totalling at most 50 points, capped by the deficit. |
| [Unload](broker_passive_damage_on_reload.md) | Talent | Static check | **Damage example**: magazine capacity 100, ammunition spent 30: 2% + ⌊30 ÷ 10⌋ × 2% = 8%, so base Damage of 100 becomes 108. With an existing 25% bonus at the same stage, 100 × (1 + 25% + 8%) = 133. |
| [Swift Endurance](broker_passive_stamina_grants_atk_speed.md) | Talent | Static check | **Timing example**: 5.8 current Stamina counts as 5, granting 10% Attack Speed. An accelerable 1-second action becomes 1 ÷ 1.1 ≈ 0.91 seconds, rather than a direct 10% reduction to 0.9 seconds. |
| [Ramping Backstabs](broker_passive_ramping_backstabs.md) | Talent | Static check | **Power example**: 5 stacks grant 50%; Power of 500 entering this stage becomes 500 × 1.5 = 750. With another 20% Power bonus at the same stage, 500 × (1 + 20% + 50%) = 850. |
| [Moving Target](broker_passive_increased_ranged_dodges.md) | Talent | Static check | **Count example**: a weapon with 3 Effective Dodges gains 1, giving 3 + 1 = 4. The bonus does not increase Dodge Distance or Dodge Speed. |
| [Sample Collector](broker_passive_stimm_cd_on_kill.md) | Talent | Static check | Cooldown example: 20s remaining − 4 × 0.5s = 18s; four Chem Toxin infected kills instead leave 16s. The examples exclude resource-recovery modifiers and elapsed time. |
| [Jittery](broker_passive_improved_dodges_at_full_stamina.md) | Talent | Static check | Timing example: a 1s reset wait becomes 1 × (1 − 40%) = 0.6s. At 4 maximum Stamina, at least 4 × 75% = 3 points enables the bonus. |
| [Critical Chance Boost](base_crit_chance_node_buff_low_1.md) | Talent | Static check | Chance example: 10% + 5% = 15%; an initial 25% becomes 30%. The bonus adds five percentage points. |
| [Melee Damage Boost](base_melee_damage_node_buff_medium_1.md) | Talent | Static check | Damage example: base 100 Melee Damage becomes 110. With another 25% at the same stage: 100 × (1 + 25% + 10%) = 135. |
| [Potent Tox](base_toxin_power_boost_1.md) | Talent | Static check | Power example: 500 × 1.1 = 550 before the Toxin Damage curve and enemy armour. This is not a fixed +10% final Damage increase for every tick. |
| [Sticky Hands](broker_passive_reduce_swap_time.md) | Talent | Static check | Swap example: 1 ÷ 1.4 ≈ 0.71s; Spread example: 2 degrees × 0.7 = 1.4 degrees. Recoil modifies unsteadiness accumulation and recovery; actual camera displacement also depends on weapon curves. |
| [Calling for a Time Out](broker_passive_reduced_toughness_damage_during_reload.md) | Talent | Static check | Damage-reduction example: 100 × 0.75 = 75 Toughness Damage. With another 20% at the same stage: 100 × (1 − 20% − 25%) = 55. |
