Citizens of Tertium beware. The enemy has a message for you. It is seditious talk of treason. It is half-truths and complaints. Pay it no heed. Remember your vows to the Emperor. Remember also that spreading rumours is a crime punishable by death. Constrict your thoughts to what needs to be done, for blessed is the Mind too small to doubt.
