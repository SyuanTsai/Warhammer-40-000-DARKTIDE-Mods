Life without faith is mere existence beneath the all-consuming shadow of heresy, without so much as a candle to hold that darkness at bay. It is the fate of some to pass from this life as mere kindling, whereas mine is to light the flame. And I see that I am needed in this place, at this hour. For is not said that if ten thousand feckless souls burn in the flames of perdition to awaken the fervour of one righteous man, then the Emperor rejoices? Aye, it is.
