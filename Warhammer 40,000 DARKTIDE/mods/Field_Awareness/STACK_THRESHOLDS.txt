The user rejected the initial lowest-source thresholds (Burn/Soulblaze 3,
gas Toxin 6). Final choices: Burn 12, Soulblaze 12, Bleed 16, Toxin 30.
Flash inclusively at the selected threshold and above. These are display
thresholds, not restrictions on gameplay or changes to native stack caps.

Burn: 12, tier-IV Infernus. Infernus tiers I-IV have caps 3/6/9/12, so
6 is the next blessing tier above 3, not 12. Twelve was chosen explicitly.
Incendiary shotgun has a 10-stack application cap; it is not overlooked,
but the chosen 12 threshold intentionally will not flash at that cap.
The native flamer_assault effect cap is 31, retained for icon growth.

Bleed: 16. Ordinary bleed native cap is 16; bleed_long caps at 18.
Weapon blessings use application ceiling 31, limited by the effect's 16.
Per-hit stack awards are not caps. Class talents add to the bleed pool.

Chem Toxin: 30, the needle-pistol damage pool cap. Its toxin hit effects
resolve to neurotoxin_interval_buff/2/3, each max_stacks and max_stacks_cap
30. Weapon modes/critical hits/weakspots change stacks awarded per shot,
not this accumulated cap. All three damage variants are read.
The gas controller's toxin_max_stacks 6 is intentionally not the selected
threshold. Its single controller stack is not a damaging toxin stack.
The separate toxin armour-rending buff caps at 15 and shares keyword toxin
but is class buff rather than interval_buff: exclude it from DOT counts.
