FIELD AWARENESS 1.7.0 - INSTALL / UPDATE
Creator: joERodman. Accepted for upload on October 23, 2026.

1. Close Darktide and back up your current mod folder and saved preferences.
2. Extract this ZIP. Copy its mods/Field_Awareness folder into your game's mods
   folder and choose Copy and Replace. You can also extract into the game
   root so the lowercase mods folder merges with the existing folder.
3. Keep Field_Awareness enabled exactly once in your existing mod_load_order.txt.
   Preserve entries for your other mods. DML and DMF are required.

When switching from a versioned testing folder, retire that older folder
outside the game's mods folder and replace its load-order entry with
Field_Awareness. Do not load both copies. The public folder and manifest use
the stable Field_Awareness name for subsequent Copy and Replace updates.

We're attempting to preserve your settings for future updates. DMF stores
preferences separately, and retaining your choices is the intended workflow;
this is not a guarantee for every future release. Keep a backup before updating.
This archive contains no personal saved settings or private load order.
